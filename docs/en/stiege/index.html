Step by step to the ÖSD exam.

Same structure, same task types, same time as the ÖSD exam. The texts are new, the format is the same.

What Stiege does

All four parts

Reading, listening, writing and speaking for levels A1, A2, B1, B2 and C1.

Like the real exam

Task types, order and timing follow the official model tests. Exam timer if you want it.

A daily plan to your exam date

Enter your exam date and Stiege plans every day. Missed days are spread out, without penalty.

Feedback on writing and speaking

AI rates your texts by the exam criteria and shows what is missing.

Structure, not memorising

You learn how a text is built and fill it with your own sentences.

Austrian topics

Standard German, as the exam requires, with words and topics from Austria.

Why “Stiege”?

In Vienna, a staircase is called a “Stiege”. Like climbing the stairs, you reach your goal one step at a time: passing the exam.

Try it for free

A1 reading and listening are completely free. In all other parts and levels you can try tasks for free. After that: €7.99 a month, €21.99 for 3 months or €54.99 a year. No ads.
